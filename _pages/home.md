We study how individuals respond physiologically to perturbations, especially meals, and what these responses reveal about biological regulation and opportunities for personalized nutrition. We analyze time-series data, integrate multi-omics, build new cohorts, and develop AI models. We welcome students from both directions: nutrition and physiology trainees who want to explore computational opportunities, and quantitative scientists (statistics, CS, engineering) drawn to questions in human metabolism.
